10 cosy things to do in York this winter

Our favourite is the candlelit ghost walk, which leaves from the Minster steps at 7.30pm every Friday and Saturday from 1 November until the end of February. Tickets are £12 on the night.

The Christmas market runs from 14 November to 22 December on Parliament Street, free to wander.

For something warmer, the tea room on Stonegate does afternoon tea from £24 a head; book ahead at weekends.
